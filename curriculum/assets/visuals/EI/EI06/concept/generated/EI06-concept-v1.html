Building an MVP

Text equivalent and source

Choose the smallest experiment that tests a consequential assumption. Before building automated room matching, manually coordinate a small authorized group using a shared schedule. Measure successful bookings and effort to learn whether coordination solves the problem. An MVP tests a consequential assumption; it need not automate the whole product.
