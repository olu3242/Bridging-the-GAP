What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Text equivalent and source

Distinguish learned prediction from fixed rules and human judgment. A campus room booking limit can be enforced by a fixed capacity rule. Classifying free-text maintenance requests may benefit from a trained model, but uncertain cases still need a person. Choose the rule for capacity because its decision criterion is explicit. Automation may follow fixed rules. Select a method from the task, evidence and cost of error.
