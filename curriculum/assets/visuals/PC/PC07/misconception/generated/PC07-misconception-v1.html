Context Engineering

Text equivalent and source

Correctly reject this misconception: Sending all available data always improves an assistant response. A tutor explaining an objective needs approved lesson content and the learner attempt, but not another student submission or a hidden answer key. Filter before constructing the request. Permission and relevance determine what enters context. More data does not create authority.
