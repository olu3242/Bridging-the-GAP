Human Approval & Guardrails

Text equivalent and source

Place approval before consequential side effects and recheck its scope. An organizer approves a message for 20 recipients. If the agent changes the recipient list to 200, the old approval is insufficient. Bind approval to the reviewed proposal and require another decision. A materially changed proposal needs fresh approval before any consequential side effect.
