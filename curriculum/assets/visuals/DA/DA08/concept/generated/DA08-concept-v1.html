Data Visualization

Text equivalent and source

Choose a chart that supports a specific comparison honestly. A bar chart starting its axis near the smallest value exaggerates a small completion-rate difference. Use an appropriate baseline and show the rates and cohort sizes explicitly. Dramatic appearance is not accuracy. Explain the baseline and retain rates, cohort sizes and uncertainty.
